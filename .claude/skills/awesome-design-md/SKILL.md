# Awesome DESIGN.md

`design-md/<product>/DESIGN.md` holds one product's design system: colour tokens, type scale,
spacing, components, motion and do/don't rules. Read the two or three closest to the job, take the
principles that fit, and build an original system from them. Never copy a brand's name, logo or
signature colours into another product.

Good starting points for a data-dense finance product: `linear.app`, `mercury`, `stripe`,
`vercel`, `raycast`, `coinbase`, `kraken`, `revolut`, `posthog`.
